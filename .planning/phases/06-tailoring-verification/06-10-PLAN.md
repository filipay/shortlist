<objective>
Close the two failed check-4 blockers (VERIFICATION truths 6 and 7; ESC-06-A / CR-01 residual and ESC-06-B / WR-01) by REPLACING the fragile lexical region anchor with a structural discriminator a body bullet cannot satisfy, and by making an unanchorable region fail closed visibly — satisfying orchestrator criteria A–E.

A bullet in an earlier entry that names a later entry's org AND title ("Partnered with Acme's Senior Engineer team to cut 5 incidents") still hijacks that later region, donates the earlier digit as a surplus candidate, and HARD FAILS an ordinary correct CV — with no recovery, because D-118's only recovery is a fresh tailor that deterministically re-runs the same predicate, so preview/download are dead for that CV. In the opposite direction, when no single line carries both needles (empty/all-punctuation title, or a long header that wraps), `region_token_report` emits `candidates=[]` (`app/verify/numbers.py:249-264`) and `check_metric_containment` skips the region (`app/verify/text_checks.py:86-88`), so a fabricated digit inside it passes the one blocking integrity check silently. Round 2 (06-08) narrowed a fragile mechanism and shipped a test that named the org but not the title, so it could not fail; this plan replaces the mechanism and pins the failure class.

Output: an `_entry_lines` whose region start is gated on the entry's date-range needle on the candidate line or its immediate successor (with non-empty org/title/dates needles), a `check_metric_containment` that returns a visible `warn` for any unanchored region while an anchored surplus still hard-fails, the committed `org_title_collision_extract.txt` literal-repro fixture, the literal, class-property and fail-closed regression tests, and the `VERIFIER_VERSION` 1.0.3 provenance bump.
