Close the still-FAILED truth 6 (check 4 false-fails a valid CV) and satisfy criterion E by making the region anchor a structural, org-first boundary and by preventing an unanchored region's digits from leaking into its predecessor.

Purpose: Check 4 is region-scoped (D-113): every surviving digit core must be grounded in its own region's source facts, and only a `fail` blocks (D-114). `_entry_lines` (`app/verify/numbers.py:159-178`) gates a region start on the entry's org AND title appearing anywhere on a line (`org_needle in line`) plus date-range adjacency. Because the only recovery (a fresh tailor, D-118) re-fails, preview and download are dead for that valid CV. `06-VERIFICATION.md` (status `gaps_found`) reproduced this two ways and `06-REVIEW.md` raises it as WR-02 (Warning) on changed file `app/verify/numbers.py`; criterion E (`06-10-PLAN.md:225`) re-opens the fix until a scoped re-review reports no unresolved critical/warning finding on the changed files.
