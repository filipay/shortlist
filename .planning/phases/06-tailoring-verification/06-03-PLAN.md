<objective>
Deliver the review step the phase's second success criterion names: the user sees the tailored CV and its change log — including anything dropped and why — before any LaTeX or PDF exists, and the compile they then trigger is refused if the CV it was generated against has moved.

Purpose: "review before compiling" is what keeps the human in the loop at the moment that matters, and the deep-clone of the change log is what makes TAIL-08 honest rather than decorative. The round-tripped patch is a trust boundary: it is client-controlled, so it must be re-parsed and re-grounded server-side — safe by construction, because even a hand-crafted patch has no text field and therefore cannot fabricate.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| review form (`patch_json`) → compile route | Client-controlled JSON crosses into the patch engine |
| review form (`base_cv_version_id`, `fact_store_hash`) → guard | Client-controlled guard values cross into the concurrency check |
| `RenderPlan` + patch reasons → HTML preview | Audit/model text crosses back into rendered HTML |

| T-06-13 | Tampering | forged/edited hidden `patch_json` | high | mitigate | Re-parsed with Pydantic `extra="forbid"` and re-grounded by `apply_patch` at compile; the schema has no text field, so a forged patch can only reorder/reweight/demote/drop/re-order. |
| T-06-14 | Tampering | stale-patch TOCTOU | high | mitigate | `verify_original_matches` recomputes the semantic `facts_hash` from the loaded facts and compares `cv_repo.current_version()`; a mismatch is a 409 refusal with no artifact written.
