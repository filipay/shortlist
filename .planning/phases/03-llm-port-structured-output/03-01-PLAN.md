<objective>
Prove the thinnest real end-to-end path: **one schema-constrained call that actually completes through
`opencode serve` and returns a validated typed result** — then wire that proven path to a single
operator-facing entry point.

Purpose: PLAT-02 and PLAT-05 both rest on a port that can do a structured call at all. Until this
plan, `app/llm/` has health and discovery only, `OPENCODE_MODEL` is dead config, and `llm_calls` is an
unwritten table. This plan makes the call real, makes the model resolution real, and gives the
operator a way to observe it — so Phases 5 and 6 have a backend to call.

Output: `app/llm/contract.py`, `app/llm/builders.py`, the extended port and server backend,
`app/domain/schemas/probe.py`, the `llm_calls` writer, `POST /settings/llm-test` with its result
surface, and the three new `Settings` ceilings.

**MVP framing — user story (MVP_MODE active):** **As** the job seeker running this locally, **I want
to** press one button that proves my own opencode backend can answer a schema-constrained call and
tell me exactly what happened, **so that** I know the ranking phase has a real backend before I trust
any ranking output.

**Covers:** success criteria 1, 2 (the classified outcome), and 4 at the port level; PLAT-02; and the
request-side vocabulary PLAT-05 pins.
</objective>
