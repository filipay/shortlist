<objective>
Fix the one FAILED must-have truth (#23): the queue's "Ranked X of Y" strip draws its numerator and its denominator from disjoint populations, so it can literally read "Ranked 30 of 5". Make the strip a ratio of one run's own population, and pin that behaviour with tests.

Purpose: the strip is the phase's own expression of D-89/D-93 ("ranked X of Y — cap reached"). A progress surface that reports a false ratio is a transparency defect on the Core Value screen: the user cannot tell how much of the day's queue was actually scored.

Output: `RunSummary` carries the run's own candidate count; `_ranking_strip` uses it; the template degrades honestly when it is absent; the obsolete strict-xfail sentinel is retired and replaced by two first-class regression tests in the queue suite.
</objective>
