<objective>
Close G-07-5 and G-07-6: the tracker row must wrap its long note (no page-level horizontal scroll) and its scoped action cell must stop painting the `dd/mm/yyyy` date field over the `Update status` button.

Purpose: the tracker row reused the queue's non-wrapping cell vocabulary on a row shape it was never sized for. `.truncate` (`white-space:nowrap`, no width) makes the note column demand the note's full max-content width; `.listing-actions { min-width:10rem; white-space:nowrap }` makes the last column demand the sum of both labelled edit forms; and because `.queue-table` is `display:block; overflow-x:auto`, that forced width renders as a horizontal scrollbar with the Actions column off the right edge. The same `white-space:nowrap` inherited into the shrink-to-fit `inline-block` `.listing-action` forms suppresses the soft wrap between a `width:100%` `select` and the following button, so form 1's `Update status` overflows its box and is painted over by form 2's `<input type="date">`. The queue is unaffected because its action forms hold only hidden inputs and buttons (D-132 / UI-04).

Output: a phase-scoped `tracker-*` cell vocabulary (per 07-UI-SPEC.md:34) that wraps, a wrapped note cell that keeps its `title=""`, the regenerated stylesheet, and a layout-contract regression test.
</objective>
