<objective>
Close WR-01: honour `tailoring_enabled` on `POST /listings/{id}/cv/compile`, consistently with the page, the generate route and `tailoring_skip_reason()`.

Purpose: `app/config.py:193-201` documents that `tailoring_enabled=False` makes the tailoring surface refuse before any model call or filesystem read — zero `llm_calls` rows, zero `cv_artifacts` rows. Three of four surfaces honour it, but the compile route never checks it: a round-tripped `patch_json` POSTed straight at `/cv/compile` still loads the fact store, renders, compiles and writes a `cv_artifacts` row while the operator has turned tailoring off. The off switch is the operator's cost and privacy control; a route that ignores it is the switch not existing.

Output: the guard in `compile_tailored_cv`, its docstring naming the D-119 contract, and the tests that pin the refusal, the zero-row/zero-file outcome, the cross-surface parity, and the fact that the switch stays live in both directions.
</objective>
